# Privacy Policy: Hajj & Umrah Companion

**Effective date:** 4 November 2026
**App:** Hajj & Umrah Companion for Android (`com.personal.hajjumrah`)
**Developer:** Simple Islam
**Contact:** [simpleislamapps@gmail.com](mailto:simpleislamapps@gmail.com)

Hajj & Umrah Companion guides you through Hajj and Umrah step by step, with authentic duas, their sources, the 40 Durood and a Tawaaf and Sa'i counter. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## In short

- The app does **not collect, send, sell or share** any personal or sensitive information.
- There is **no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking**.
- The app works **fully offline**: the guide, the duas, their sources and the fonts are all built into it.
- Your settings and counts are stored **only on your phone**.

## What the app stores on your phone

So that it can work, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

- your Tawaaf, Sa'i and dua counts
- the step or dua you were last reading, so you can carry on where you left off
- your settings: the colour scheme, the Arabic and English text sizes, and whether bead sound, vibration and transliteration are on

This information does not leave your phone through the app, and we (the developer) cannot see it.

## Permissions

The app asks for two permissions:

- **Vibration**, for the gentle pulse you feel as you count each round of Tawaaf or Sa'i, or each bead of a dua counter. You can turn this off in the app's Settings.
- **Internet**, which Android requires for the page viewer the app is built on. The app loads everything from inside itself and makes no network connections, so it does not use this to send or receive anything.

It does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, notifications or any other access.

## Backups

If backup is turned on for your phone (for example Google backup), Android may include the app's saved settings and counts in that backup, so they come back when you set up a new phone or reinstall the app. Android makes this backup in your own Google account, under Google's privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

## Sharing with third parties

None. The app has no third-party code that collects data: no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social media tools. Its fonts are built into the app, so it does not download them.

## Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you, we do not share them, and we will delete them if you ask.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your settings and counts stay on your phone until you remove them. You can delete them at any time by:

- tapping **Settings > Counters > Reset all** in the app (for the counts),
- clearing the app's storage (phone **Settings > Apps > Hajj & Umrah > Storage > Clear storage**), or
- uninstalling the app.

We never receive your data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete.

## Security

The app keeps your settings and counts in its private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The app makes no network connections, so it never transmits your data.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
