Hajj & Umrah Companion

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

In short: Hajj & Umrah Companion does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

About the app

Hajj & Umrah Companion is an Android app that guides you through Hajj and Umrah step by step, with authentic duas, their sources, the 40 Durood and a Tawaaf and Sa‘i counter. It works fully offline: the guide, the duas, their sources and the fonts are all built into the app.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not create an account for you.

Information kept on your phone

To remember your preferences, the app saves a small amount of information in its own private storage on your phone:

  • your settings, such as colour scheme, text size, sound and vibration;
  • your Tawaaf, Sa‘i and dua counts;
  • the step you were last reading, so you can carry on where you left off.

This information never leaves your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet and third parties

The app does not send or receive any personal information over the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media services, and no third-party code that collects data.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for the gentle pulse you feel as you count each round of Tawaaf or Sa‘i and each bead of a dua counter.
  • Internet: required by Android for the app's built-in page viewer. The app does not use it to collect or send any information.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or the app, please contact the developer through the email address listed on the app's Google Play page.
